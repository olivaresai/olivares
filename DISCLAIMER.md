## 7. What this software actually does, and the risk that comes with it

This is stated plainly, because it is the real risk of this product and not a formality:

**Olivares AI is a control plane. It decides whether an action by an AI agent is allowed or
blocked, and it records evidence about what happened.** That places it in the path of things you
care about, and both directions of failure are real:

- **A wrong or overly strict configuration can block legitimate work and interrupt production.**
  Deny-closed behaviour is deliberate where it is wired: when the control plane cannot decide,
  it refuses. A misconfiguration, an unreachable dependency, an expired credential or a defect
  can therefore stop agents, pipelines, jobs or services from doing their work.
- **A wrong or overly permissive configuration can let through exactly what you meant to stop.**
  A policy that does not match, a connector that is not wired, a collector that is silently
  failing, an unenforced seam, or a signal the software never received, all produce the same
  visible result: no alert, no block, no finding — and an action you believed was prevented.
- **What the software does not observe, it cannot report.** Coverage and attribution carry
  explicit tiers, and an absence of findings is never proof that nothing happened.
- **Evidence is derived from what the software was given.** Inventories, mappings, control
  statuses, drift panels and evidence packages are derived aggregates. They can be incomplete or
  wrong if the inputs are incomplete or wrong.

You are responsible for deciding whether this software is fit for the environment you intend to
run it in, and for testing both directions of failure before you rely on it.

- **The notice** is carried in every source file of the covered material, on the line after its

  // Additional terms under AGPL-3.0-only section 7(a) disclaim warranty and limit liability: see DISCLAIMER.md at the repository root.

[`NOTICE`](NOTICE), [`LICENSING.md`](LICENSING.md) and [`README.md`](README.md) also point here.

citing, in support, the four lines that say otherwise. Placement is the whole of enforceability
here: terms that are not validly added are not additional terms at all, and section 7's final
paragraph lets a recipient strip a "further restriction" that arrives without them.
