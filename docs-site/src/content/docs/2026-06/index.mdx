## What is this?

Olivares AI is the **control plane for the AI agents already running on your
infrastructure**: it **discovers** every agent, model, MCP server and tool in your
estate, gives you a **read/write access map** of what each one can reach, lets you
**operate and govern** the fleet from one cockpit, and diffs the access each is
*Permitted* against the access it is *Observed* using — all **self-hosted**, so your
data never leaves your boundary. Audit and compliance are the bridge for when your
security team comes asking.
