The documentation is organized by [Diátaxis mode](/2026-06/start/how-the-docs-are-organized/)
— tutorials, how-to, reference, explanation — because that is how docs stay
usable. But people arrive with a *role*, not a mode. These are the four
paths through the same pages, in reading order.

## Platform engineering — install it, operate it

You own the deployment: uptime, backups, upgrades, capacity.

1. **See it work:** the [quickstart](/2026-06/start/quickstart/) (five minutes), then
   the [zero-to-graph tutorial](/2026-06/tutorials/zero-to-graph/) if you want the
   guided version.
2. **Install for real**, by scenario:
   [single node (systemd)](/2026-06/tutorials/getting-started/single-node/) ·
   [Docker Compose](/2026-06/tutorials/getting-started/docker-compose/) ·
   [Kubernetes/Helm with HA](/2026-06/tutorials/getting-started/kubernetes/) ·
   [air-gapped](/2026-06/tutorials/getting-started/air-gapped/).
3. **Wire signals:** [connect a source](/2026-06/how-to/connect-a-source/), then the
   per-connector guides ([pgAudit](/2026-06/how-to/connectors/pgaudit/),
   [CloudTrail](/2026-06/how-to/connectors/cloudtrail/),
   [eBPF/Tetragon](/2026-06/how-to/connectors/ebpf-tetragon/)).
4. **Operate:** [monitoring & SLOs](/2026-06/how-to/monitor-with-prometheus/) ·
   [backup & restore](/2026-06/how-to/backup-and-restore/) ·
   [troubleshooting](/2026-06/how-to/troubleshooting/).

Reference you will live in: [configuration](/2026-06/reference/configuration/),
[CLI](/2026-06/reference/cli/), [connectors & tiers](/2026-06/reference/connectors/).

## Security engineering — govern it, contain it

You own least privilege, enforcement and incident response for the agent
estate.

1. **The model first:** [what the access map is](/2026-06/start/what-is-olivares-ai/)
   and [honesty & limits](/2026-06/start/honesty-and-limits/) — what the product
   will and will not claim.
2. **Govern:** [govern and approve](/2026-06/how-to/govern-and-approve/) (RBAC, the
   restrict-only PDP, recorded decisions), then the recipes:
   [deny-closed policies](/2026-06/how-to/cookbook/deny-closed-policies/) ·
   [HITL approvals](/2026-06/how-to/cookbook/hitl-approvals/) ·
   [drift triage](/2026-06/how-to/cookbook/drift-triage/).
3. **Enforce on the agent surface:**
   [Claude Code hooks & the PEP](/2026-06/how-to/connectors/claude-code-hooks-pep/) ·
   [MCP governance](/2026-06/how-to/connectors/mcp-governance/) ·
   [budgets as guardrails](/2026-06/how-to/cookbook/budgets-and-finops-guardrails/).
4. **Contain:** the [kill-switch drill](/2026-06/how-to/cookbook/kill-switch-drill/),
   and [harden the deployment](/2026-06/how-to/security-hardening/).

Deep background: the [security model](/2026-06/explanation/security/security-model/)
and [threat model](/2026-06/explanation/security/threat-model/).

## Compliance — evidence, audit, residency

You answer to auditors and regulators; you need records that prove
themselves.

1. **What the evidence is:** the recorded-decisions guarantee in
   [govern and approve](/2026-06/how-to/govern-and-approve/#the-human-in-the-loop-posture)
   — the append-only, signed ledger under every decision.
2. **Get it out:** [push it to your SIEM](/2026-06/how-to/cookbook/push-to-siem/),
   or [pull/tail it](/2026-06/how-to/forward-audit-to-splunk/) for WORM archival —
   and verify it offline
   ([troubleshooting § ledger](/2026-06/how-to/troubleshooting/#the-ledger-fails-verification)).
3. **Keep it survivable:** [backup & restore](/2026-06/how-to/backup-and-restore/) —
   continuity-safe bundles, the off-box key rule, the restore drill.
4. **Residency and provenance:**
   [air-gapped operation](/2026-06/tutorials/getting-started/air-gapped/) ·
   [verify a release](/2026-06/how-to/verify-a-release/) ·
   [open core & licensing](/2026-06/explanation/open-core-and-licensing/).

The honest baseline to cite in assessments:
[Honesty & limits](/2026-06/start/honesty-and-limits/) — what runs, what is
on-demand, what is design-stage.
